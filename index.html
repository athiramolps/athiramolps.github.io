My Story

I started my story in digital marketing over three years ago while exploring how websites attract traffic and gain visibility on search engines. As I worked with SEO, content optimisation, and website performance, I developed a strong interest in understanding how search engines rank pages and how strategic improvements can drive organic growth.

Over time, I built strong skills in keyword research, competitor analysis, and data-driven SEO strategies. Seeing websites improve their rankings and organic traffic strengthened my interest in analysing search behaviour and creating content that aligns with both user intent and search engine algorithms.

Currently, I am a Digital Marketing Intern at Intelercs in the UK, where I continue to expand my experience in SEO strategy, search analysis, and content optimisation, focusing on helping websites grow organically and reach the right audience.
